Alertness Detection System

Real-time alertness monitoring

A computer vision tool that watches a face through the camera and flags when a person looks drowsy.

  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • Flask
  • MediaPipe
  • SciPy
  • Render

Highlights

  • Real-time alertness monitoring using pretrained facial landmark detection
  • EAR and MAR calculations on MediaPipe Face Mesh landmarks
  • Served through a Flask app built on OpenCV video processing
  • Also known as the Student Drowsiness and Yawning Detection System: it streams browser webcam frames to a Flask backend
  • Leaky frame counters ignore normal blinks, and a live 0 to 100 focus score drops during fatigue and recovers while focused
  • Plays an audio alarm, and offers motivational quotes and study tips to help students refocus
  • Deploy-ready on Render with Gunicorn, running CPU-only with no custom model training

How it works

MediaPipe finds the facial landmarks and my own rule-based layer decides what they mean. EAR below 0.21 for 5 frames flags drowsiness, MAR above 0.55 for 4 frames flags yawning, and the focus score moves by -3, -2 or +1 per frame.

Next up

Per-user calibration, time-based thresholds, head-pose detection, session analytics and on-device inference.

Bank Customer Churn Analysis

Data analysis and prediction

An end-to-end study of 10,000 bank customers to find who churns, why, and which segments the bank should retain first.

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • NumPy
  • Matplotlib
  • Seaborn
  • scikit-learn

Highlights

  • Covers data cleaning, exploratory analysis, five business-driven questions, a baseline prediction model and retention recommendations
  • Found Germany churns about 32%, roughly double France and Spain, and customers in their 50s are the most likely to leave
  • Inactive members churn at about 27% versus 14% for active members, and customers with 2 products are the most loyal
  • Female customers churn about 25% versus 16% for male customers, and churned customers hold higher average balances
  • Logistic regression and decision tree baselines reach roughly 85% accuracy; since about 80% of customers stay, recall and ROC-AUC matter more

How it works

Clean the data, explore correlations and outliers, answer five business questions with grouped statistics and charts, then train baseline models and turn the findings into recommendations such as targeting inactive customers aged 40+.

Next up

Gradient boosting with proper imbalance handling, customer segmentation and an interactive dashboard.
